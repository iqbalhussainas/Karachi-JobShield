How to Identify Fake Job Advertisements

Learn how to recognize suspicious recruitment offers and protect yourself from job scams.

1. Never Pay Registration Fees

Be careful if a recruiter asks for money to confirm a job or arrange an interview.

2. Verify Company Information

Check the company's official website, contact details and business information.

3. Be Careful of Guaranteed Jobs

Be suspicious of advertisements promising guaranteed employment without a proper selection process.

4. Protect Personal Information

Never share passwords, banking details, OTP codes or sensitive identity information with unverified recruiters.

5. Verify Email and Contact Details

Check whether the recruiter's email address belongs to the company's official domain.

6. Avoid Suspicious Links

Do not open unknown links or download unexpected files from job advertisements.

Remember

Always verify a job opportunity through official company channels before sharing personal information or making payments.
